Resolution robustness on 80 held-out KiTS23 cases after choosing an 8 mm perturbation on 20 development cases. (A) Each line joins the original and degraded 3 mm segmentation Dice for one case; horizontal jitter is cosmetic, diamonds mark medians, and the dashed line is the fixed Dice < 0.83 bad-segmentation threshold. (B) Spearman correlation between clean-minus-degraded Dice and degraded-minus-clean score, with saved 95% bootstrap intervals. Positive correlation supports sensitivity to deterioration; the primary interval includes zero. The primary score is mean absolute 3 mm–6 mm organ-probability disagreement near the predicted organ; binary entropy is secondary. (C) Saved G1 review curves for the mixed original/degraded queue, with volume, random, and oracle references. AUC measures failure discovery, not classification of degraded images. The oracle uses true Dice only as an evaluation upper bound. The 160 views come from 80 cases; mixed-queue bootstrap samples retain both views together. Intervals come from the original 2,000 bootstrap resamples; random is the mean of 1,000 orders. No new model selection or statistical analysis was performed for this figure. Scope is resolution degradation only; lower-resolution, original, and mixed queues have different bad-segmentation prevalence, so their raw AUCs have different oracle ceilings.
