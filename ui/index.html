    Before you start

    An AI has marked the kidneys in CT scans in red. It makes mistakes. Your task is to correct the red marking so that it covers the kidneys exactly. First a short guide, then a practice scan that does not count.

    What to mark

    • The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs at the back of the belly, one on each side of the spine. In these scans they are bright, often with a brighter rim.
    • A tumour is a round lump that grows from the kidney. It often looks darker or patchy. It counts as kidney: mark it too. So do cysts (round, dark and even).
    • You look at the patient from the feet: the patient's right side is on the left of the screen (marked R).
    • A scan is a stack of slices. Look at the slices above and below too: mistakes are often at the top or bottom end of a kidney.

    How to use the trackpad

    • Paint: press the trackpad down and keep it pressed while you move your finger (or hold it down with your thumb and move another finger). The circle shows the size of the brush.
    • Add paints kidney that is missing. Erase removes red that is not kidney. Undo (or ⌘Z) takes back the last stroke.
    • Change slice: the ↑ ↓ arrow keys, the ▲ ▼ buttons, or drag the slider on the right.
    • You have minutes per scan. Press Done when you are happy. When the time is up, the scan is locked and saved.
