## How to write the notes

- **Distill, do not copy the log.** [PROJECT_LOG.md](../../PROJECT_LOG.md) already has every number. The notes say what
  the numbers mean, what they do not show, what went wrong, and what matters most.
- **Every number has its interval (if there is one) and the path of the file it comes from**
  ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), section 2: never invent or estimate results).
- **Say what is locked or fixed beforehand and what is exploratory.**
- **Keep it short.** The report is at most 5 pages for the whole project, so each part gets well under a page. The
  section "Top 3 points" decides what goes in first.
- Update the line "Last updated" every time you change the file.
