# Consent to take part in a user study

**Project:** Where to Look First: Prioritizing Human Review of AI Medical Image Segmentation\
**Course:** DTU 02830 Advanced Project in Digital Media Engineering, group 2 (Bjørn, Otto, Rasmus, Frederik)\
**Contact:** [contact e-mail]

## What the study is about

An AI program outlines organs in CT scans, and it sometimes makes mistakes. We want to find out whether showing
where the AI is uncertain helps people correct its mistakes faster.

## What you will do

On a laptop, you correct the AI's outline of the kidneys in 6 CT scans, after a short guide and a practice scan.
Each scan has a time limit of a few minutes. After every three scans you answer a short questionnaire about how
demanding the task was (NASA-TLX). The whole session takes about 40 minutes. You do not need any medical knowledge.
The scans come from a public research dataset of anonymous patients and are not used for any patient's care.

## What we save

- your participant ID (for example P07), not your name;
- the times and actions in the program (for example when you started and finished a scan, brush strokes, which
  tools you used);
- the outlines you corrected;
- your questionnaire answers;
- technical details about the browser and screen size.

We do not record video or sound, and we do not save your name or e-mail address with the data. Your name is only
on this form, which is kept apart from the data and does not show your participant ID.

## How the data are used and stored

The data are used only for our course report and presentation. Results are reported as averages or anonymous
examples, never in a way that shows who took part. The data are kept on the group's computers and deleted when
the course has ended (after the exam in December 2026).

## Taking part is voluntary

You decide whether to take part. You can stop at any time during the session without giving a reason, and it has
no consequences for you. After the session, you can ask us to delete your data until [date] by sending us the
participant ID on your slip.
